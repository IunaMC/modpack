# IUNA

**IUNA** is the modpack of the **IUNA** community: Minecraft **1.21.1** on
**NeoForge**, bringing together technology, magic, exploration and plenty of
food in a world made for playing together. It was built for the community
server, but you can install it and play on your own.

Players get **more than 280 mods**, all required, plus 2 resource packs and 7
shaders.

> **About the server:** the IUNA server is private and open only to community
> members. This pack does not grant access to it. If you are already a member,
> the instructions are in the community channels.

## Installation

**CurseForge app:** click **Install** on this page, or search for "IUNA" in the
Minecraft modpacks tab.

**Prism Launcher:** *Add Instance → CurseForge*, search for "IUNA" and pick the
version. You can also download the `.zip` from *Files* and use *Add Instance →
Import*.

The launcher installs NeoForge and downloads the mods for you.

**To update,** the safest way is to create a new instance with the new
version. If the first number changes (from 3.x to 4.0, for example), the
server world is new as well. To keep your worlds, screenshots and settings,
copy the `saves`, `screenshots` and `config` folders from the old instance.

## Requirements

- **Java 21.** The CurseForge app and Prism download it for you.
- **Memory:** we recommend allocating **8 GB** of RAM to the game (6 GB
  minimum). With shaders or many visual mods, 10 GB. Your computer also needs
  spare RAM for the system.
- **First launch:** with almost 300 mods, the first load takes a few minutes.
  That is normal.

## Singleplayer

The pack works in local worlds. Mods that run only on the server (world
generation, administration and performance) are not installed for players, so
a local world generates different terrain and structures from the server.

## Versions

The version number follows `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`:

- **MAJOR:** new world or full reinstall.
- **MINOR:** a mod was added, removed or updated.
- **PATCH:** nothing changes for the player (configuration, documentation).
